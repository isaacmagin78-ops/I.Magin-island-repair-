# Trend Watch — the thing that was supposed to exist from day one

**Created 2026-08-16.** Isaac, in his own words, on why:

> *"When we started this, it was to get everything in order so I could stay ahead
> and you guys would be searching trends so we would be able to be better and
> bigger and best — and we missed Jack Dorsey news because I was too busy trying
> to fix you guys."*

He is right. **No session has ever watched anything.** Nine months in, the way he
found out that Jack Dorsey's Block had shipped Buzz — an open-source system for
coordinating AI agents, launched 2026-07-21, squarely in his lane — was that his
friend Scott sent it to him, roughly three weeks late.

That is the failure this file exists to end. The system was supposed to buy him
time to stay ahead. Instead it consumed the time and watched nothing.

## What gets watched

Five lanes. Each one has a reason and a named consequence for missing it.

### 1. AI agent infrastructure — *the lane he actually got beaten in*
Block / Jack Dorsey, Anthropic, OpenAI, Google. Specifically: anything that
changes how a **non-technical person coordinates several AIs at once.** New agent
protocols, memory/context products, multi-agent tooling, MCP-adjacent releases.

**Why:** this is the mission's own subject matter. Being late here is being late
to his own thesis. Buzz is the proof.

### 2. Creator monetization mechanics — *the gates he is stuck behind*
TikTok Shop seller rules and commission rates. YouTube Partner Program
thresholds, including the Shorts track. Amazon Associates commission changes and
link-placement policy. Instagram / Threads monetization.

**Why:** four separate routes are currently closed to him (TikTok Shop deferred,
YouTube Partner 18.3/4,000 hours, Amazon Influencer 1/10 sales, and one affiliate
link total). Every one of those thresholds is a number a platform can change
without telling him. A rate change or an eligibility change **is money**.

### 3. Real-estate marketing AI — *the premise of the Listing Content System*
Compass, Sotheby's, Berkshire Hathaway, eXp, Zillow. What the big brokerages are
handing their agents, and when.

**Why:** the entire pitch to Linda rests on giving her something her brokerage
does not already mail her every day. The day her brokerage ships it, the pitch
changes. Known dates already on file: Compass Q1 2027, Maestro Mar 2026.

### 4. The two creators he follows — *named repeatedly, never once checked*
**Sabrina Ramonov** (`@sabrina_ramonov`) and **Dan Martell** (`@danmartell`).

**Why:** Isaac has said many times that their guidance is what this system should
follow. A repo-wide grep for either name once returned zero hits. Nobody has ever
looked at what they published. This is the cheapest lane and the most neglected.

### 5. Pet / rescue content — *his actual niche*
What is working right now in rescue-dog and pet short-form. Format shifts,
platform pushes, seasonal hooks.

**Why:** the same twelve clips have cycled for a month and reposts lose about
17%. Knowing what format is working is worth more than another repost.

## Rules for the watcher

1. **Report the date something happened, not the date you found it.** A finding
   with no date is a rumor. This is the same rule that governs every other file
   here.
2. **Every item gets a source link.** If it cannot be sourced, it is labeled
   `unsourced` and reported anyway — a rumor he can check beats silence.
3. **Say "nothing this week" out loud.** A quiet week is a finding. Silence is
   indistinguishable from the watcher being broken, and that ambiguity is exactly
   how five days of stopped publishing went unnoticed.
4. **Rank by what it changes for him**, not by how interesting it is. A TikTok
   commission change outranks a model release he cannot use.
5. **Three items maximum per run.** He does not need a newsletter. He needs the
   thing he would otherwise have missed.
6. **Never recommend a tool switch.** The mission is that these systems work
   together. Report what shipped; do not tell him to move.

## Where findings go

Per the Ike OS rule — *no research asset exists until it is written down*:

1. A dated block appended to **`HANDOFF.md`**, so the next Claude Code session
   reads it automatically.
2. The same block into **Ike OS in Notion**, which is the canonical record.
3. Anything that changes a number in **`PROJECT-BRIEF.md`** gets corrected there
   in the same run, with the new date.

**Nothing gets reported to Isaac in chat and nowhere else.** That is how the last
nine months of findings evaporated.
