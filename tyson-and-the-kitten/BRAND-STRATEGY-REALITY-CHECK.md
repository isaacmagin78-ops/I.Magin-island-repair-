## What the strategy gets right (confirmed by our own data)

1. **Story-first, no hard sell** — the doc's Phase 1 rule ("no selling until 1,000
   engaged followers") matches what the analytics already show: emotional
   rescue-story posts outperform $19-pitch posts on every metric.
2. **One lesson, not fourteen** — the manuscript simplification to
   "You don't have to become what people expect you to be" is correct and
   should drive the v2 manuscript edit.
3. **The kitten is the breakout star** — the top three posts by views all
   feature Miss. The merch instinct (plush kitten) is supported.
4. **Real Tyson + illustrated Tyson dual engine** — unique, defensible, and the
   AI-animation-backlash mitigation (always lead with the real dog) is sound.

## Where the strategy contradicts reality (and the fix)

1. **"Set up accounts" (Phase 0) is already done.** TikTok, IG, YT, Threads
   exist and post daily via Blotato. Skip to the parts of Phase 0 not done:
   character lock, reference sheets, email capture.
2. **The doc's Phase 1 rule is being violated today.** Nearly every scheduled
   caption (Aug 1–3 queue included) hard-sells the $19 kit. Fix: move the kit
   to pinned comment + bio only; captions go story-only.
3. **The First 30 Days Kit is missing from the doc's revenue ladder entirely** —
   yet it is the only live product. It monetizes a different audience (adult
   rescue adopters) than the book/plush ladder (kids/parents). Keep it as the
   adult-facing funnel while the character IP builds. Do not wait 16 weeks for
   the book to have something to sell.
4. **Unverified checkout comes before everything.** $0 lifetime revenue plus a
   403 on the kit page means the funnel may be a locked door. No strategy
   matters until a stranger can complete a $19 purchase. Test incognito on
   mobile; check Vercel Deployment Protection on the hosting account.
5. **Licensing/Bluey math is a north star, not a plan.** Character licensing
   conversations require six-figure follower counts. Correct order by expected
   dollars-per-effort right now: working kit funnel → YouTube monetization at
   500 subs → book launch → merch ladder → licensing.
6. **"Give the kitten a name" — she has one.** Miss (the real kitten). Locked
   in the manuscript. No change.
