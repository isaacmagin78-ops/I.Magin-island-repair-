The call

I.MAGIN, and nothing after it

Luxury houses don't put the job in the name. Aman doesn't say resorts, Hermès doesn't say leather. The name is the name; the category sits underneath in small type — which is exactly where CONCIERGE goes, and it can be swapped for a city, a division, or dropped entirely without touching the mark.

Everything is lowercase-weight, not loud. Heavy type and wide gradients read as streetwear or sports. Thin strokes with wide letterspacing is what money looks like — it's the same move on a Bentley badge, a Tom Ford box and a Miami penthouse listing.

Why it looks expensive
MaterialChampagne gold with a real sheen crossing it — a surface, not a color. That's the difference between expensive and colorful.
RestraintOne accent in the entire identity: the period, in Miami coral. Everything else is metal and black.
SpaceThe letterspacing is doing most of the work. Confidence reads as room, not volume.
MotionA nine-second sheen and a slow pulse. You notice it on the second look, never the first — which is the point.
MiamiPresent as one note, not a theme. Coral against obsidian is South Florida. A full sunset gradient is a t-shirt.
