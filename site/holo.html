What you're looking at

Real light, not a picture of light

This isn't an image or a video. Every frame is computed on your screen right now — a shader running on your phone's graphics chip, the same way a game renders. The iridescence flows because a mathematical field is flowing underneath it. Tilt-shimmer, spectrum split, a light sweep passing through the letters.

Which means it never repeats and it never pixelates. Same file works on a phone, a billboard, or the side of a building. That's what a hologram-looking mark actually is: not a picture — a program.

The mark is still your name. I.MAGIN — Isaac Magin, and imagine. The light is just what makes someone look up.
