# Market log — every market read, kept

**Started 2026-08-18, on Isaac's go-ahead.** Companion to `ROOM-LOG.md`.

## Why this file had to be created

The `market-read` skill already said to save every read — into `TREND-WATCH.md`.
**It never happened once.** `TREND-WATCH.md` is about the creator and AI lane; a market
read on a yacht club has nothing to do with it, so the rule was never followed and the
reads evaporated exactly like the room reads did.

A rule that points at the wrong file is the same as no rule.

## This log works differently from ROOM-LOG, on purpose

**Room knowledge keeps. Market numbers rot.** A membership figure from August is worthless
by December, but *how that industry actually works* is still true years later.

So every entry splits in two:

- **DURABLE** — how the business really runs, who the players are, the structural pressure,
  what an owner actually worries about. Still true next year.
- **PERISHABLE** — this year's numbers, prices, counts. **Stamped with a re-check date.
  Never repeat a perishable line to a real person without re-verifying it first.**

The second durable thing is the list itself: **which businesses and venues Isaac asked
about is a map of his real pipeline.** That stays valuable long after the figures die.

## How to add an entry

Newest at the bottom. Leave blank what isn't known — a blank field is honest.

```
## [Business / industry] — read [YYYY-MM-DD] · re-check after [YYYY-MM-DD]

**Why he asked:** who he was standing in front of, or what prompted it
**Location:**

### The six lines, as delivered
THE NUMBER
THE DIRECTION
THE PRESSURE
THE OPENING
THE QUESTION

### DURABLE — still true next year
- how this business actually runs
- who the players are
- the structural pressure that isn't going away
- what the owner actually worries about

### PERISHABLE — re-verify before repeating
- [figure] · source · year

**⭐ Isaac's correction:** what he said back. He has thirty years around these businesses
and the research does not. HIS WORD OVERRIDES THE RESEARCH. Record it verbatim.

**What happened:** did the conversation go anywhere? Did the question land? Was the
pressure line right? Filled in later, if he says.
```

## Read before writing an entry

- **Never invent a figure.** Not a market size, not a growth rate, not a member count.
  `unsourced` is an acceptable label; a made-up number is not.
- **Every number carries a source and a year**, in the log as well as in the answer.
- **Local beats national.** Broward and Palm Beach before any national figure.
- **If there is no honest opening for Isaac, write that down too.** "Not his market" is a
  real finding and saves the next session from re-deriving a forced angle.
- **Isaac is the authority on these rooms and these owners.** Do not argue him out of a
  correction; record it.

## What to do with it later

- **A pressure that shows up across three or more different businesses is a product.**
  Note repeats at the bottom — that is the whole point of keeping this.
- **Mirror anything that changes strategy into Ike OS in Notion.** The log is the record;
  Notion is where decisions live.
- **Do not publish this.** Same rule as `ROOM-LOG.md` — it is working material, not content.
