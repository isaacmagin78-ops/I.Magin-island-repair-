# The Collection Record — pilot offer

A fixed-fee documentation package for people whose houses are full of things
nobody has ever written down. Built directly on `collection-inventory.csv`,
`concierge_tools.py` and `renovation-dashboard.csv`.

## Who it's for

A 65–85 year old South Florida homeowner with decades of acquired pieces and no
record of any of it — usually triggered by a renovation, a move, a downsizing,
or an estate-planning conversation that just got real. They do not want an
appraiser and they do not want a liquidator. They want someone to make the pile
legible before someone else has to.

## What I deliver

- **On-site photo pass.** Every significant piece, consistent lighting, one
  master image plus detail shots, named to a fixed convention.
- **One spreadsheet.** Every item with location, condition, provenance status,
  valuation status, and a recommended path — hold, appraise, consign, private
  sale, donate, or legal-check-first.
- **A one-page action sheet.** What to insure this month, what needs a
  valuation, what to leave alone. Generated, not hand-written, so it stays
  consistent as the list grows.
- **Story sheets for the top ten pieces.** The acquisition story recorded while
  the person who remembers it is still here to tell it. This is the part
  families actually keep.
- **One handoff call** with whoever needs it — spouse, adult kids, the estate
  attorney, or the listing agent.

## How I charge

Flat fee, quoted up front, no hourly meter — this client does not want a clock
running in their house, and a fixed scope protects my calendar.

| Scope | Fee |
|---|---|
| Up to 50 items, one residence | $3,500 |
| 50–150 items, one residence | $6,500 |
| 150+ items or multiple residences | Quoted |
| Live renovation dashboard, monthly add-on | $1,250/mo while the build is active |

The renovation dashboard is the natural attach: anyone spending $2M+ on a
renovation has to decide what goes back on the walls, and that decision needs
the inventory.

**To Mom.** "Nobody but you knows where the gold weights came from. I'm going to
photograph everything, write your story down next to each piece, and hand you
one spreadsheet that says what to insure, what's worth selling, and what nobody
should ever touch. Two afternoons, and then it's done and it's permanent."

**To Arturo.** "You have real assets sitting across three buildings with no
schedule, no photographs and no valuations. If something burns or walks, the

the record — photographed, located, condition-noted, with a recommended action
on every line. Fixed fee, two weeks, no meter."

**To Linda.** "When your seller is 78 and leaving a 6,000-square-foot house, the
contents are what stalls the closing. I document the collection and tell them
what conveys, what ships, what sells before the move. You look like the agent
who solved the worst part of the job. Send me the listings you dread."

## The 30-day pilot

One home. Mom's. Deliberately the easiest access and the best stories.

- **Hard cap: 25 items.** Not the whole house. The cap is the product.
- **Two on-site sessions, four hours each, not on consecutive days.**
- **Fixed end date** written down before the first session. No open-ended
  "while you're here, can you also look at…" — that is how this eats a month.
- **Deliverable:** the spreadsheet, ten story sheets, one action page.
- **Then it becomes the sample.** A real deliverable about real objects, shown
  to Linda and Arturo, beats any pitch deck. Second engagement is priced at the
  full $3,500.

**Guardrail:** nothing on-site scheduled August 7–11. The pilot works around
that week, not through it.
