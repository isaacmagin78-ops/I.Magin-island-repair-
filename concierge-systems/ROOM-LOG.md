# Room log — the corpus

**Started 2026-08-18, on Isaac's go-ahead.** This file is the point of the whole
concierge business. Everything else is delivery.

## What this is

Every time the `room-read` skill runs, the answer gets written here. Not a summary —
the actual read, plus what Isaac corrected, plus what actually happened if he says.

Thirty years of knowing how these rooms work has been leaving through a hole: the skill
answered, the conversation ended, and nothing was kept. This file is the hole closed.

## Why it matters more than it looks

1. **It is the only thing here that cannot be bought.** The AI tools are rented; anyone
   has them in six months. This is the asset.
2. **Undocumented know-how is legally nothing.** Trade-secret protection requires the
   knowledge be set down "with reasonable particularity." Writing it here is the act that
   turns thirty years of experience into something that can be valued, licensed, insured
   or sold. Carrying it only in his head means there is no asset at all.
3. **It is what eventually answers without him.** One person cannot be reachable ten
   minutes before every door. A large enough corpus can be.

## ⛔ Never publish this

This is the trade secret, not marketing. Publishing it as content destroys the thing that
makes it valuable — the 2026 evidence is consistent that published knowledge commoditises
while the same knowledge applied as judgment carries a premium. **Excerpts go to a paying
client, never to a feed.** No client is ever named without their authorization.

## How to add an entry

Newest at the bottom. Copy the block, fill what is known, leave the rest blank — a blank
field is honest and a guessed one is not.

```
## [Venue or event] — [YYYY-MM-DD]

**Type:** club · gala · dinner · opening · showing · boat show · pitch · meeting · other
**City:**
**Occasion:**

THE DOOR
WEAR
THE ROOM
CURRENT
DON'T

**Confirmed vs inferred:** what came from the venue's own site or a recent source, and
what was pattern-matched from similar rooms. Label every line.

**⭐ Isaac's correction:** what he said back. HIS WORD OVERRIDES EVERYTHING ABOVE — he has
thirty years in these rooms and the research does not. This field is the most valuable
line in the entry; it is where the moat actually enters the record.

**What actually happened:** filled in afterwards, if he says. Was the door read right?
What was missed?
```

## Read before writing an entry

- **Isaac is the authority.** When he contradicts what was researched, record what he says
  and do not argue him out of it.
- **Never guess a dress code.** "I could not confirm it, here is the safe version" beats a
  confident wrong answer that gets someone turned away.
- **Blank beats invented.** An empty field is a finding.
- **Patterns are the product.** After enough entries, the repeated rules across rooms are
  worth more than any single entry. Do not tidy them away — note them at the bottom.
