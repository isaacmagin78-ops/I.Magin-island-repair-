## Hard rules — these are not preferences

1. **Never fabricate a number, a review, a price, or a contact detail.** Empty
   brackets `[N]` ship instead. An honest blank beats a plausible fake.
2. **Never generate property images.** AI-made interiors of a *real* listing,
   captioned with that listing's real specs, under a licensed broker's name, is
   a misrepresentation problem. The shoot is the client's input, not our output.
   Say it out loud in pitches — it's a differentiator.
3. **Real people require real authorization.** Nothing publishes under anyone's
   name without their say-so.
4. **Verify before you claim.** Never call something working, broken or done
   without checking it live, and say the date out loud. A finding is only as
   fresh as its date.
5. **Don't hand Isaac homework.** If a thing can be done for him, do it.
6. **Plain language.** No jargon. If a term is unavoidable, define it in the
   same sentence.
7. **This repository is public.** No health, legal, financial or family details
   in any file here. That belongs in Notion.
