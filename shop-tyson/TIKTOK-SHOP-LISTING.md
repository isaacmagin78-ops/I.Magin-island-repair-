## 4. What to tap — Seller Center, on the iPad

Plain steps. Any term you might not know is explained in the same line.

1. **Open** `seller-us.tiktok.com` in Safari and sign in as **tysons time**.

2. **Tap your account icon → Account Settings → Qualification Center.** This is the page
   where TikTok checks you're allowed to sell a particular brand or category.

3. **Tap "Brand Qualification" → "Add Brand Authorization." Type CRAVE.** Watch what the
   screen offers you:
   - If it offers **"Proof of Purchase"** — you're through. Photograph the pallet-store
     receipt and upload it. Go to step 5.
   - If it only offers **"Letter of Authorization"** — that's a signed permission letter
     from Mars Petcare, the company that owns CRAVE. **You won't get one. Stop here and
     read section 5.**

4. **While you're in Qualification Center, look for "Pet Supplies."** If it asks you to
   qualify for the category, do it. You are reselling sealed retail bags — you didn't
   make, import, or repack them — so the FDA registration requirement should not apply to
   you. If TikTok asks for it anyway, that's a real stop; don't invent a document.

5. **Same screen, find "Tax Information."** Confirm the **W-9** (the IRS form that lets a
   company pay you) shows as submitted and accepted. If it doesn't, fill it in now —
   otherwise your money sits in TikTok and won't come out.

6. **Photograph the product.** One unit, white surface, near a window, 4 shots: front,
   back with the ingredients panel, the best-by date readable, one with your hand for
   scale.

7. **Go to Products → Manage Products → Add New Product.**

8. **Upload the photos.** Front of package first — that's the one shoppers see.

9. **Paste the Product name** from section 2. Replace every `[bracket]` by reading the
   actual bag. Don't guess a size or a date.

10. **Set Category** — Pet Supplies → Dog → the food or treats option TikTok shows you.

11. **Set Brand to CRAVE.** Not "No Brand."

12. **Paste the Description** from section 2. Same rule — fill the brackets from the bag.
    Leave out the "Tyson eats this one" line unless it's true.

13. **Paste the bullet points.**

14. **Package weight and size.** Put one unit on a kitchen scale, in the box you'll ship
    it in, and enter what it says. TikTok charges shipping off this number — guessing low
    costs you money on every order.

15. **Set Price.** Fill in the worksheet in section 2 first, using the real fee percentage
    that Seller Center shows you.

16. **Set Stock** to the number of units you physically have. Count them.

17. **Tap Submit.** TikTok reviews it — usually hours, sometimes a day. You'll get an
    email either way.

18. **Once it's live:** open TikTok, go to your profile, and check the **Shop tab** shows
    the product. TikTok's own email says ~70% of shoppers buy through the Shop tab first.

19. **Then go live.** 30 hours of LIVE built this entire following and earned 147 diamonds
    while two months of scheduled posting moved nothing. Hold the bag up on a live and say
    what it is. That's the format that works for you — don't switch to a format that doesn't.

## 5. If CRAVE is blocked at step 3

Then the honest answer is: **you do not have a product you can list on TikTok Shop today**,
and no amount of copywriting fixes that. Here's why each alternative is out, so nobody
re-proposes them:

- **The First 30 Days Kit ($19 PDF)** — digital goods on TikTok Shop US need the **Virtual
  Goods** category, which launched May 2026, is **invite-only**, covers only gift cards /
  game codes / software licences, and requires automatic API delivery. Ebooks and courses
  are excluded. It cannot be listed.
  *Source: [TikTok Shop digital products policy 2026](https://www.z.media/insights/how-to-sell-digital-products-on-tiktok-shop)*
- **Tyson's own gear** — the duck, the football, the Dolphins collar, the Beautyrest bed.
  You own one of each. They're Tyson's, not stock. There's nothing to ship.
- **Any other branded pallet stock** — same brand-authorization gate as CRAVE.

**The one route that clears every gate: a product under your own brand.** You'd be the
brand owner, so authorization is you submitting your own paperwork, and there's no
letter to chase. That is a real build, not a paste — so it belongs in a separate
conversation, not this file.

**Do not go quiet on TikTok while this is unresolved.** The shop being empty is not the
same as the shop being dead, and the LIVE format still works with no product listed at all.
