Why I'm a fan

I make films. I made two for Legends Ranch in July 2026, and cutting them is how I got close enough to the place to have an opinion about it.

Three things stuck.

The Purple Heart Hunt has run since 2003. Not a campaign, not a season — the same thing every year for combat-wounded veterans, sponsored, by invitation. Anybody can do a thing once. Twenty-plus years is a different kind of claim.

The school tours are free. The Wildlife Center on the property is a separate 501(c)(3), and Michigan kids walk fifteen conservation stations through 24,000 square feet of it at no cost. Free is easy to say and expensive to run.

The founder didn't start hunting until his fifties. Then he spent decades at it and ended up with the largest private collection on the continent. I have a lot of time for a late start.

None of that is my language — it is on their own pages and in the local press, and I checked each piece before writing it down. What I couldn't check, I left out. There's a list of what I cut at the bottom.

What's actually true about the place

Every line below links to where I found it. If it isn't sourced, it isn't on this page.

  • Legends Ranch is in Bitely, Michigan — Newaygo County, along the Manistee National Forest. It was established in 1998.

    Source: legendsranch.com/about

  • The property runs to roughly 2,000 acres of managed habitat — pastures, hardwoods, food plots and ponds.

    Source: legendsranch.com/about · wildlifecentermi.org

  • It is a trophy whitetail operation, and also runs turkey and pheasant hunts. The Presidential Whitetail is the flagship, and each hunter is paired with a personal guide.

    Source: legendsranch.com · legendsranch.com/hunts/presidential

  • The lodge is around 6,000 sq ft with eight private suites, an open dining hall and fireplace over a private lake, and a chef on site. Pontoons, kayaks, hiking and a supervised shooting range come with it.

    Source: legendsranch.com — the lodge

  • The Purple Heart Hunt for combat-wounded veterans has run since 2003. It is sponsored, by invitation, and ends in a dedicated ceremony. There is also a Youth Challenge Hunt for youth with disabilities.

    Source: legendsranch.com/the-hunts · Herald Review · SCI Foundation

  • The Wildlife Center on the property is a separate 501(c)(3) nonprofit, established 2018. It is a 24,000 sq ft facility holding more than 3,000 taxidermy animals — described as the largest private collection on the continent.

    Source: wildlifecentermi.org · legendsranch.com/wildlife-center · Michigan Enjoyer

  • The Center's school tours are free and sponsored — an hour inside the museum across 15 conservation curriculum stations, plus a 1.1-mile nature trail outside.

    Source: wildlifecentermi.org/our-programs · UpNorthLive

  • It was founded by Arthur Gutierrez, Sr., an adult-onset hunter who didn't start until his fifties and spent the decades after that building the collection.

    Source: wildlifecentermi.org/about · Michigan Enjoyer

  • There is a taxidermy studio on site — The Studio at Legends Ranch — so trophies don't have to leave the property.

    Source: legendsranch.com/studio · thestudioatlegendsranch.com

What I left off, on purpose. No prices, no package details, no hunt success rates, no trophy scores, no visitor or student counts, no reviews and no quotes from anyone at the ranch. I either couldn't source them or found the sources disagreeing with each other, and a fan page is not a good enough reason to guess about somebody's real business.
