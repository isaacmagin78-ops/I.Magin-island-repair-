## Keywords — 7 slots

Deliberately avoids words already in the title and subtitle. Repeating them is a
wasted slot; Amazon already indexes the title.

| # | Keyword string |
|---|---|
| 1 | `3-3-3 rule adopted shelter pet` |
| 2 | `new puppy owner gift adoption present` |
| 3 | `pet parent journal logbook record book` |
| 4 | `dog planner organizer notebook write in` |
| 5 | `adopt dont shop foster care supplies` |
| 6 | `first week new dog settling in` |
| 7 | `gift for dog mom dog dad adopter` |

**Why `3-3-3 rule` is slot one:** it's the phrase adopters already know and search —
three days to decompress, three weeks to settle, three months to feel at home. It has
real search volume and almost nothing in print targets it directly.

**Do not use:** any breed-specific rescue org name, "best," "#1," "bestselling," or the
word "training" — we explicitly position away from training, and mismatched keywords
generate returns and one-star reviews.

## Back matter — the part that actually pays

The last page of the manuscript is the highest-value page in the book, because it's the
only place a $6.69 Amazon customer can become a $19 direct customer.

It must contain:

- A link to the full digital kit at `tysons-time-kit.vercel.app`
- A genuinely free offer worth claiming — printable full-size trackers, since a
  paperback can't be re-printed when a page fills up. That's a real reason to click,
  not a bribe.
- The Instagram handle `@tysonstime`
- A plain, non-incentivized review request

**Do not** offer anything in exchange for a review, or ask specifically for positive
reviews. Both are bannable. "If this helped, a review helps another adopter find it" is
allowed and is all we need.

## After publishing

  it's the sample-spread images that turn a browser into a buyer.
