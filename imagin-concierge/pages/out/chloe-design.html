Why a page beats a posting

A job posting puts a small studio in a queue behind every company with a recruiting budget, and buys back a stack of applicants who applied to two hundred other places the same afternoon.

A link does the opposite. You text it to the one person you already have in mind, or hand it to someone at an event, and they see the actual work, the actual terms, and a way to reply — in about twenty seconds, on their phone, before the moment passes.

Nothing on this page claims anything about the studio. The role, the terms and the pay are Chloe's to fill, and until she does they stay visibly blank.
