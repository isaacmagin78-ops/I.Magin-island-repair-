Why in-building beats a search result

A handyman found online quotes a callout, a drive, and a window between eleven and three. A handyman on the fourth floor is up in ten minutes and there is no drive to pay for.

That is not a small advantage. It is the whole business, and it only works if the people in this building know he exists — which today they mostly don't.

This page fixes exactly that, and the QR board downstairs does the rest. Nothing here states a licence, a rate or an insurance status: those are Marc's to give, and a handyman page that guesses at them is worse than no page at all.
