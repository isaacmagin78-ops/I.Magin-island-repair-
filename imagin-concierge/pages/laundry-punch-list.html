Resolved — it's a Miele ventless pair.

That answers both of the things that would have wrecked tomorrow. Miele's compact machines run on a standard 120-volt outlet, so the plug in the closet is right. And the dryer is a heat pump, so there is nothing to vent — no duct needed. The rough-in already in that closet matches what the machines want.

The one Miele thing that catches people: the condensate.

A heat-pump dryer pulls water out of the clothes and has to put it somewhere. There are two ways: plumb the condensate hose into the standpipe, or leave it collecting in the internal tank and empty that tank by hand every couple of loads. Plumbing it is a five-minute job for the installer while they are already there, and nobody thinks about it until they are gone. Decide before they leave.
