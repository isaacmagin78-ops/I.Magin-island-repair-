That outlet looks like a standard 120-volt plug.

A normal electric dryer needs a 240-volt, 30-amp outlet with a big three or four-prong face. If tomorrow's dryer is a regular electric one, it will not plug into what is on that wall — that is a wasted delivery and an electrician. If it is a ventless combo or a gas dryer, 120V is correct and you are fine. Check the model tonight.

There is no dryer vent in either photo.

A conventional dryer has to vent outside through a four-inch duct. Nothing like that is visible in the closet. Either the dryer is ventless — heat pump or condenser — or a duct exists somewhere out of frame. A vented dryer with nowhere to vent does not get installed.
